<!--problem-->

The client wanted to increase organic traffic to the online store’s commercial pages.

These problems stood in the way:

- Existing pages needed on-page optimization: writing and updating copy, meta tags, etc.
- The site structure needed to be expanded, with new optimized pages created
- Page templates needed to be reviewed and refined, both to help search engines understand and index them and to improve user behavior metrics
- Technical errors on the site

<!--solution-->

- Competitor and site structure analysis, with suggestions for expanding the site and creating new pages
- A technical audit, preparing specs for developers, and monitoring the implementation
- A technical audit for AI Overviews and LLMs (GEO/AEO) and specs for developers
- A comprehensive audit (conversion and trust factors, usability, E-E-A-T) and specs for developers
- An audit of existing content for uniqueness, length, and signs of AI-generated text
- On-page optimization: creating new pages with copy, updating and rewriting existing copy, and ongoing work on H1 headings and meta tags
- In parallel, off-page optimization:
  - Analyzing the backlink profile and disavowing low-quality referring domains
  - Building a link plan for priority pages, selecting target sites, and placing links to the site
  - Placing naked-URL links (crowd links and directory submissions)
  - Analyzing the Google Business Profile and SERM (ORM), and preparing recommendations

<!--result-->

After on-page optimization of existing pages, expanding the structure, fixing some of the technical errors, and working on the backlink profile, organic traffic to commercial pages grew 20%. That is almost 11K more visits per month on a site over 21 years old that already had a sizable search audience. Rankings for the relevant keywords in Google also improved.

<!--limits-->

Some of the recommendations and specs provided were not implemented, or were implemented with long delays, so the pace of growth was lower than it could have been:

- Usability, conversion, and trust factors: reviews with markup, internal linking sections, FAQ, advanced filters, etc.
- Technical: XML sitemap, category pagination, structured data, page speed, etc.
- Content and structure: slow content production; limited expansion of the structure (in particular, parameter-based filter URLs stayed closed to indexing, although some already existed on the site and could have been used); and no blog written despite a ready content plan and briefs for copywriters
- A limited link building budget: competitors were placing links on far more expensive sites

These results came only from the changes that were actually implemented; the spec items that remain unimplemented are untapped potential for further growth.
